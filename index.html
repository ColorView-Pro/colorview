About

Hello to everyone who opened this to know about us. We are 2 normal students, Nour Eldeen and Ahmed Sameh. We wanted to build something that helps the community, so we made ColorView Pro to help colorblind people and the people designing for them. You can pick colors with HEX, RGB and HSL, simulate 8 types of color blindness with adjustable severity, check contrast and safe palettes, and preview your own images and even your camera through each mode. We used AI tools to help us learn and move faster while building this. Thank you for trying it out — more updates are coming soon.

— Nour Eldeen & Ahmed Sameh
